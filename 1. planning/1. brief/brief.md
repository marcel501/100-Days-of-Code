# INTRODUCTION

This is a learning focused project with heavy involvement in practical elements. Its scope is applied software development towards engineering.




## BACKGROUND

As a student of engineering, the mission is to develop a sufficient understanding of software development workflows elements and methodologies as to gain a sufficient professional basis necessary for DevOps and other computer engineering focused roles. The heavy involvement in engineering (mainly mechanical) is important for the upkeep of professional knowledge from the result of graduating from a mechanical engineering discipline, and for more fundamental understanding of necessary computer aided systems which are present for this discipline.
